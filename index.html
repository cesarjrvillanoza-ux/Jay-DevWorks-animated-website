About me

Hi, I'm Jay DevWorks. I turn rough ideas into working websites.

I'm a freelance web developer who builds websites and web apps for small businesses, startups and individuals. I work directly with you from the first call to launch day, so there are no account managers and nothing gets lost in translation.

I care about the parts visitors feel: pages that load quickly, layouts that work on a phone, and forms and checkouts that stay out of the way. I also care about the parts you feel later: clean code, sensible hosting and a site you can update without calling me every time.
